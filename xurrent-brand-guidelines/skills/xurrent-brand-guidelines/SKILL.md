## 1. Brand Attributes & Personality
Xurrent is a platform built on **integration, collaboration, efficiency, and agility**. 
* **Energy Flow:** Pulsating energy and electric connections.
* **Productivity Surge:** Effortless team synchronization.
* **Trust Circuit:** Reliable and empowering service delivery.
* **Sharp Spark:** Excellence and sharp workflow execution.

## 2. Color Palette
The palette uses vibrant, flowing colors to suggest energy in motion. It includes core colors along with an expanded palette of supporting tones (analogous shifts, tints, and shades) to facilitate UI states, hover effects, gradients, and accessible typography.

### Primary Brand Colors
* **Electric Violet:** HEX `#7800D3` | CMYK 85-100-0-0 | Pantone 266 C
    * *Usage:* Main branding, headlines, and key UI elements.
    * *Supporting Tones:*
        * **Deep Indigo:** HEX `#3100D3` (Analogous - Blue-shifted) | Ideal for high-contrast text links or button hover states.
        * **Vibrant Magenta:** HEX `#BE00D3` (Analogous - Pink-shifted) | Introduces dynamic energy into illustrations or gradients.
        * **Bright Amethyst:** HEX `#9F20FF` (Tint - Lighter) | Excellent for active menu items or hover states on dark backgrounds.
        * **Rich Plum:** HEX `#4C0086` (Shade - Darker) | Provides depth for subtle borders or text overlay without using absolute black.
* **Dark Purple:** HEX `#300352` | CMYK 85-100-0-40 | Pantone 2627 C
    * *Usage:* Main branding, headlines, and key UI elements.
    * *Supporting Tones:*
        * **Midnight Navy:** HEX `#150352` (Analogous - Blue-shifted) | Perfect for Dark Mode components or heavy footer backgrounds.
        * **Deep Wine:** HEX `#4A0352` (Analogous - Red-shifted) | A warm, rich dark tone for multi-layered card components or shadows.
        * **Royal Velvet:** HEX `#5B059B` (Tint - Lighter) | Serves as a smooth gradient transition bridge between Dark Purple and Electric Violet.
        * **Absolute Void:** HEX `#040008` (Shade - Darker) | Ultra-dark base for deep contrast or interface backgrounds.

## 3. Typography
Xurrent uses a hierarchy of three specific Google Fonts. If Space Grotesk or Raleway is not available, use Inter in its place.

### Primary: Space Grotesk
* **Usage:** Headlines, subheads, and infographic callouts.
* **Weights:** 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 700 (Bold).

### Secondary: Raleway
* **Usage:** All body copy and long-form text.
* **Weights:** 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semi-bold), 700 (Bold).
* **Color:** Default to Dark Gray (`#333333`).

### Monospace: DM Mono
* **Usage:** Technical data points, code snippets, or specialized UI labels (e.g., in the ROI calculator).
* **Weights:** 400, 500.

## 4. Visual Elements & Style
* **Iconography:** Soft, organic-shaped icons inspired by nature. **Line weights must be thin and light.**
* **Graphics:** Flowing visuals with smooth shapes. Imagery should emphasize "fluid motion" and "energetic precision."
* **Composition:** Prioritize white space with specific "pops" of color. Avoid large heavy blocks of dark color.
* **Formatting:** Use **Bold text** for "phrases to land" and key metrics.

## 5. Logo Usage
* **Preferred Layout:** Wide (Horizontal) layout.
* **Clear Space:** Defined by the distance between the Clover mark and the "Y" in the wordmark.
* **Trademark:** The ™ symbol must always be present and never modified to ®.
